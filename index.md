# about

I am Nathaniel Tunggal, a current senior at [Williams College](https://www.williams.edu/) majoring in [Chinese](https://chinese.williams.edu/) and [Computer Science](https://csci.williams.edu/). I am passionate for Chinese because it allows me form intercultural connections with people across the world, shaping my perspectives and worldview. My interest in computer science stems from the intellectual challenge of problem solving as well as its wide applicability and impacts on society. Recently, I have become interested in international relations, security, and policy due to the inherent interdisciplinary nature of the field and their global impacts. I believe addressing global issues requires collaboration between people of many different backgrounds and perspectives, and my goal is to use my CS+Chinese expertise to make meaningful contributions to IR/security through objective research and analysis.

Much of my experience consists of [projects](./projects.html) in Chinese and computer science, particularly artificial intelligence and natural language processing. In Chinese, I have researched the state and perceptions of China's Social Credit System and compared US and Chinese responses to the COVID-19 pandemic. I spent a summer studying at the International Chinese Language Program in Taiwan, where one of my [papers](./writings.html) was selected for publication on the newsletter. In AI/NLP, I developed a solution to the 2025 SemEval task of detecting hallucination spans in language model outputs. I am also currently working on a senior thesis focusing on optimizing tokenization for Chinese texts. I have practical development experience as a Full-stack Development Intern at [Salesmentor.ai](https://www.salesmentor.ai/), where I implemented application features and refactored code to improve efficiency.

Outside of academics, I am a classically trained bassoonist and pianist. I play bassoon in the [Berkshire Symphony](https://music.williams.edu/ensembles/berkshire-symphony/) as well as in chamber music at Williams.
